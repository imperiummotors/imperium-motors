# Homepage Blueprint

## 1. Floating Glass Navigation

### Objective

Create a navigation that feels like entering a private automotive atelier.

### Layout

- Floating glass navigation bar
- Centre-aligned Imperium Motors crest and wordmark
- Hamburger menu on the left
- Private Enquiry button on the right
- Rounded pill navigation
- White translucent glass
- Soft backdrop blur
- Hairline border
- Soft ambient shadow
- Fixed while scrolling

### Experience

The navigation should never dominate the page.

It should feel calm, refined, elegant and effortless.

The logo is the centre of gravity.

The navigation should quietly disappear into the architecture rather than compete with it.

---

## 2. Hero Experience

### Objective

Create a first impression that immediately communicates refinement, discretion, confidence and trust.

Visitors should feel they have entered a private institution rather than a traditional luxury dealership.

### Visual Direction

- White architectural environment
- Bespoke hero photography or cinematic video
- Natural daylight
- Calm composition
- Editorial spacing
- Premium typography
- Champagne gold accents used sparingly
- Museum-quality presentation

### Content

Headline

Where Luxury Meets Legacy

Supporting Text

Private Acquisition

Global Relationships

Discreet Concierge

Primary CTA

Request Private Enquiry

Secondary CTA

Explore The Collection

### Motion

- Soft fade-in
- Gentle parallax
- Smooth scroll indicator
- Calm transitions
- No aggressive animations
- Hero visible immediately
- Never delay content

# V2 Development Rules

From this point onwards:

- Build one component at a time.
- Never redesign two sections simultaneously.
- Every component must reach production quality before moving to the next.
- Every new component must improve upon Version 1.
- Every design decision must support the Imperium Philosophy.
- Every decision must increase trust.
- Consistency over novelty.
- Quality over speed.
