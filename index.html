The Founder's Vow

Born in 1978 in **Nellai**, Antony Paschal Selwyn's journey began not with engines, but with a childhood promise. In an era before the internet, his world was shaped by automotive magazines and a single red **Ferrari F40** toy car gifted by his father in 1985.

From the disciplined kitchens of the UK hospitality industry to the high-stakes hypercar networks of London, Antony has spent decades mastering the art of **personal relationships** and **absolute integrity**.

"In 1985, I promised to connect people through these machines. Today, Imperium Motors is that promise kept."
